## What we do

- **Product engineering for founders.** We take ideas from sketch to a running system — design, build, ship, operate. Same small senior team end to end.
- **AI workflows for regulated industries.** Internal automation for law firms, family offices, and tax advisors in the DACH region.
- **Our own products and ventures.** We build software we own and operate, and co‑found startups with strong operators — including [evmquery](https://evmquery.com) and [INTA](https://intacompliance.com), among others.
- **Open source.** Libraries we extracted from real systems and kept maintaining.
